# A mock CLI's failure-injection counter must guard on "is a threshold set"…

A mock CLI's failure-injection counter must guard on "is a threshold set" before comparing, not just default both the counter and the threshold to 0. v1.11's mock `gh` (`tests/version-collision/fixtures/mock-bin/gh`) compared `n <= MOCK_CREATE_FAIL_COUNT` to decide whether to fail a milestone-create call; with neither `MOCK_CREATE_COUNTER_FILE` nor `MOCK_CREATE_FAIL_COUNT` set (the common no-failure-injection case), both defaulted to `0`, and `0 <= 0` is true — every call failed, even though the test never asked for one. Caught immediately: two happy-path `claim_roadmap_version.py` assertions ("no milestones exist" and "milestone ahead of roadmap") failed with "5 consecutive collisions" instead of a clean claim. Fix: gate the failure check on the threshold being explicitly `> 0`, not just comparing two possibly-both-zero values. Pattern: any mock's opt-in failure-injection knob needs its own explicit presence check — a numeric default that happens to collide with "no failures requested" silently flips every happy-path test into a failure test.
