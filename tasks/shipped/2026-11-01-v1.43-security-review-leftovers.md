# v1.43 Security Review Leftovers

v1.43 Security Review Leftovers (2026-11-01, `tasks/security-review-leftovers-spec.md`): closes
the dev-platform items of `issue #131` that need no decision from the user.

## The verified problem

- The live `~/.claude/settings.json` held 311 allow entries, 207 of them not in
  `settings/settings.json`, including `kill` of specific PIDs and `sudo` entries (count from a
  python3 one-liner over both files). Removing them by hand-editing JSON was the only way.
- `settings/permissions-retract.json` could not hold them: it is tracked in a public repo, so a
  retract entry would publish the grant's text.

## What shipped

- `scripts/prune_live_grants.py`: lists each live grant not in the baseline with a class
  (`pid-kill`, `sudo`, `scratch-path`, `inline-token`, `unclassified`). `--apply` writes a
  mode-600 backup, then removes the first four classes. It never removes `unclassified`
  grants, never writes the baseline, and prints at most 100 characters of an entry. Against the
  real live file it reports 2 pid-kill, 14 sudo, 5 scratch-path, 56 inline-token and 130
  unclassified (list-only run; nothing was applied).
- `tests/prune-live-grants`: 9 PASS, including list mode writing nothing, backup contents and
  mode, baseline untouched, and `Bash(kill *)` staying unclassified.
- `settings/README.md` Hygiene section now points at the tool; `commands/merge.md` notes that a
  permission prompt on its `kill` is expected, because `Bash(kill *)` was retracted in v1.39.

## Findings

- The two `tests/install` failures in `issue #131` did not reproduce: `tests/install/run.sh`
  passed 9 of 9 in this worktree and on an extracted `git archive HEAD` copy. The cause is
  unknown.
- The spec's rough count (129 "other", 17 kill/sudo) used a looser regex than the tool's rules.
  The tool's counts are the ones that ran. Entries mentioning `kill` that stay unclassified and
  are therefore never removed: four `pkill ...` entries and `tmux kill-session *`.

## Scope honesty

- `--apply` was not run on the live file. The user reviews the list and runs it.
- `${VAR}` inside a `Bash(...)` permission pattern is still untested.
- Not done, needs the user: the sandbox trial decision, enabling the secret pre-commit hook,
  the Google Maps key, tracked `.env` files, and browser-only account checks.
